## Inconsistency findings

1. **Page frame: one header component, about fifteen different page wrappers.** `ErpPage` is used by 2 of ~60 pages (OpeningStockSummary.tsx:67, ClosingStockSummary.tsx:116). Everything else pairs `PageHeader` with a hand-written wrapper, each with its own padding and max-width: `container mx-auto space-y-4 p-0 sm:p-6` (LocationVouchers.tsx:319), `space-y-6 sm:p-6` (OpeningStockDetail.tsx:129), `p-4 md:p-6 space-y-5 w-full` (StockTransfers.tsx:145), `w-full space-y-6 sm:p-8` (OffloadDetail.tsx:238), `p-6 max-w-md mx-auto` (MySettings.tsx:107), `container mx-auto p-6` (MixBatches.tsx:211), `flex flex-col h-full overflow-hidden` (BaleLedger.tsx:401), and no padding at all (`space-y-6`: EditSupplier.tsx:141, ImportStockItems.tsx:593, POImport.tsx:476).
